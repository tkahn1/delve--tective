# Round 1: scout b ideas 12 23

Verbatim final report from this subagent.

Searches were standard web searches only. I did not search Devpost, Product Hunt or the app stores directly, and I did not search for #19 existing apps beyond one query. Where a URL is missing, nothing was found.

**#12 Choir of One** — Verdict: DIFFERENT. Closest: Harmony Voice iOS (https://apps.apple.com/app/id532501340), which turns your voice into up to 4 voices. Also Mu Voice (4-part harmony plugin), john-yeap01/harmoniser (GitHub, PSOLA, in development), and MIT AI Harmonizer (https://nime.org/proc/nime2025_84/), which is offline. Claims: Google Tone Transfer and "CREPE low latency" not found. CREPE 22M is flagged as too heavy for real time (SwiftF0 paper, https://arxiv.org/html/2508.18440v1). Red flag: use pYIN or SwiftF0, not CREPE, for the sub-150 ms goal.

**#13 Flipbook** — Verdict: EXISTS (as a tool). Closest: Illusion-Diffusion HF Space (https://huggingface.co/spaces/AP123/IllusionDiffusion), a ControlNet-QR illusion rather than a flip. Visual Anagrams (https://github.com/dangeng/visual_anagrams) is the real flip method. Claims: CVPR 2024 CONFIRMED. The official code is DeepFloyd IF only. A source says SD latent-space versions exist (Tancik's Illusion-Diffusion), and the card's SD port is plausible but unconfirmed. Red flag: DeepFloyd IF is too heavy for 8 GB without offloading (guide: https://tmzh.github.io/post/2024-01-22-running-deep-floyd-with-limited-memory). Not found: VRAM or speed numbers for SD flips.

**#14 Window** — Verdict: DIFFERENT. Closest: VincentLefevre/3D-parallax (https://github.com/VincentLefevre/3D-parallax), a head-tracked single photo with a depth map. Others: IvRogoz/Portal (https://github.com/IvRogoz/Portal, MediaPipe + three.js), True3D WindowMode (https://github.com/True3DLabs/WindowMode). Claims: onnx-community/depth-anything-v2-small CONFIRMED on Transformers.js (https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/depth-anything-v2-small), WebGPU with q4f16, about 26–100 MB, Apache-2.0. Red flag: none on the model. Mobile WebGPU support and fps on phones are not found.

**#15 Shadow Zoo** — Verdict: DIFFERENT. **#16 Swarm Screen** — Verdict: DIFFERENT. **#17 Face Duet** — Verdict: DIFFERENT. Closest: InterFACE (https://github.com/designerzen/InterFACE), which has a duet mode with 2 people on separate MIDI channels. Not found: eyebrow-pitch plus mouth-timbre across two phones. Red flag: none found.

**#18 Elastic** — Verdict: NOVEL. Closest: nothing found that does live slow-down with silence-cutting catch-up. Time-stretch building blocks exist: Signalsmith Stretch (npm signalsmith-stretch, official Web Audio build), SoundTouchJS audio-worklet, and the time-stretch npm package with WSOLA. Claims: Silero VAD in the browser CONFIRMED via @ricky0123/vad-web (https://docs.vad.ricky0123.com/user-guide/browser/), which uses onnxruntime-web and Silero v5 and is MIT-licensed. Novak 2019 was not checked. Red flag: the model plus WASM files are about 10 MB, so precache them. Silero VAD frames are about 32 ms.

**#19 Tired Test** — Verdict: DIFFERENT. Closest: a PVT app specifically was not found (I did not search the app stores). The science is real: Williamson & Feyer found 17–19 h awake is about 0.05% BAC (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1739867 is possibly the same study; not confirmed). Claims: the Dawson & Reid and Basner papers were not checked this session. Red flag: no source here converts BAC to "drinks", which depends on weight and sex. Frame it as BAC-equivalent, not drinks. One sleep-education site claim (reaction time doubles after 24 h) is unsupported.
